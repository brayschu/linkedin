01 About

Background

Accounting has a particular kind of rigor I find genuinely interesting — the discipline of working through information carefully, understanding why controls exist, and seeing how financial processes connect to organizational decisions. That interest is what drew me toward internal audit.

I'm currently a third-year student at Penn State's Smeal College of Business, pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Accounting with plans to graduate in May 2027. I've maintained a 3.91 GPA and been recognized on the Dean's List every semester since Fall 2023. This summer, I'll be joining Barclays' internal audit function as an intern — an opportunity I'm approaching as a serious first step toward a long-term career in audit and risk. After graduation, I plan to pursue the Certified Internal Auditor designation.

Outside of academics, I've worked for the Pennridge School District every summer since 2019 — starting as a custodian and later moving into a general maintenance helper role. Six years of consistent, hands-on work shaped how I approach responsibility: show up, do it well, and take ownership.
